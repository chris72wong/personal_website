# Toronto waterfront artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool by editing the user's Toronto reference and generating transparent motion layers. PNG masters remain in Codex's generated_images directory. Optimized project exports:

- `images/welcome-toronto.webp` — 1754 × 896, skyline, stars, moon, and water without baked-in text, boats, or clouds.
- `images/welcome-yacht.webp` — 700 × 311, transparent illuminated passenger yacht; transparent margins trimmed.
- `images/night-cloud.webp` — 1000 × 146, transparent painterly night clouds, also used in Education/Experience.

The name, white cursor, and white scroll arrows are live HTML/SVG. Separate clouds drift over the fixed skyline. Clipped water layers shimmer, selected lights and stars brighten subtly, and the yacht drifts through a 95-second alternating cycle with a gentle bob, wake, and reflection. Phone framing preserves the CN Tower and a smaller yacht. Motion pauses offscreen, in hidden tabs, or during project popups; reduced motion shows a still scene.

## Backdrop edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: live Toronto waterfront portfolio welcome backdrop.
Edit target: attached Christopher Wong Toronto night waterfront illustration.
Preserve the exact composition, detailed painterly style, dark teal night sky, stars, crescent moon, Toronto skyline with illuminated CN Tower and Rogers Centre, amber building windows, shoreline lights and their beautiful water reflections. Remove the Christopher Wong lettering and green cursor square, repairing the sky naturally. Remove all boats, sailboats and wakes, repairing the water. Remove every cloud, leaving clean dark starry sky in their former positions; clouds will be animated as separate layers. Keep all buildings, water, tower and moon positions unchanged. No text, UI, boats or clouds. Keep the wide 1755:896 framing.

## Transparent cloud prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent drifting cloud layer for a painterly night landscape.
Style reference: the clouds in attached Toronto waterfront illustration.
Create ONE wide low cluster of softly painted night clouds, in muted dark desaturated blue-teal shades, gently moonlit at upper edges, with an irregular wispy base and several natural rounded puffs like clouds in the reference. Wide, understated, airy silhouette with soft translucent edges. Genuinely transparent background outside the cloud, no opaque sky, stars, buildings, moon, landscape, text or other objects. Horizontal cloud cluster only, roughly five times wider than high, generous transparent edges. This layer will drift very slowly over both night city and mountain illustrations.
